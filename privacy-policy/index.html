Privacy Policy

Privacy information for visitors and customers using the ConsultKaro website and WhatsApp consultation links.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Information You Choose to Share

The ConsultKaro website does not currently create customer accounts or store enquiry-form submissions. When you call, email, or start a WhatsApp conversation, you choose what information to share directly with ConsultKaro or the relevant external service.

Technical Information

The website host and external services may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, and requested pages for security, delivery, and operational purposes.

WhatsApp and External Platforms

WhatsApp, social-media platforms, fonts, maps, and other linked services operate under their own privacy policies. Opening an external link transfers you to that provider.

How Information May Be Used

  • To respond to consultation requests
  • To understand and coordinate your property requirement
  • To maintain business and transaction records where necessary
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations
  • To protect the website and prevent misuse

Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be shared with a professional or service provider when necessary for an agreed requirement, with appropriate instruction or where required by law.
