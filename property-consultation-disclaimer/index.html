Property Consultation Disclaimer

Important limitations concerning ConsultKaro property search, documentation, registration, and management guidance.

Scope of Property Assistance

ConsultKaro provides property consultation, coordination, documentation workflow guidance, registration preparation, and property-management support based on the agreed scope.

Independent Verification Required

Property buyers, tenants, owners, and investors should independently verify title, ownership, encumbrances, mortgage records, approvals, taxes, possession, measurements, condition, authority, identity, and all transaction documents before committing funds or signing documents.

Professional Advice

General consultation is not a substitute for a formal title search, legal opinion, technical inspection, valuation, tax advice, loan sanction, or representation by an appropriately qualified professional. ConsultKaro may coordinate with professionals, but their engagement and scope should be separately confirmed.

No Guarantee

ConsultKaro does not guarantee that a property is free from disputes or encumbrances, that a transaction will complete, or that a governmental, financial, registration, or judicial authority will approve a particular matter.
