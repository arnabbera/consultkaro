About ConsultKaro

A focused property registration consultancy serving Kolkata.

Property Registration Support in Kolkata

ConsultKaro assists property owners, buyers, sellers and families with the practical documentation and coordination required for property registration. Our work is deliberately focused on registration and directly related services.

Our Registration Services

  • Property and document search coordination
  • Property agreement drafting and review coordination
  • Sale, conveyance and related deed drafting support
  • Property valuation and registration-cost preparation
  • Execution and registration appointment coordination
  • Post-registration mutation and record-update guidance

Our Approach

  • Understand the property, parties, transaction and available records
  • Create a clear document and process checklist
  • Keep ownership, authority, encumbrance and approval risks central
  • Coordinate the appropriate legal, valuation, tax or technical professional when required
  • Organise registered documents and post-registration next steps

Areas We Serve

ConsultKaro supports property registration matters across Kolkata, including Gariahat, Ballygunge, New Town, Metiabruz, Garden Reach, South Kolkata and other serviceable areas.
