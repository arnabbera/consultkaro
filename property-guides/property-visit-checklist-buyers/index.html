Property Visit Checklist for Buyers

A practical property-visit checklist covering surroundings, building condition, utilities, measurements, questions, and follow-up records.

A visit helps assess physical suitability, but it does not replace document verification, technical inspection, valuation, or professional advice.

Before the Visit

  • Confirm the exact address and contact person
  • Request basic property details and asking price
  • Carry a phone, notes, measuring tool, and requirement list
  • Visit in daylight where possible

Observe the Surroundings

Check road access, public transport, drainage, noise, commercial activity, safety, parking, nearby construction, essential services, and travel time.

Inspect the Property

Observe layout, usable space, light, ventilation, dampness, cracks, plumbing, electrical condition, doors, windows, common areas, lifts, fire access, and visible maintenance needs.

Ask Practical Questions

Ask about age, occupancy, maintenance, recurring charges, parking rights, water, power backup, repairs, possession, included fixtures, and the reason for sale.

Record and Compare

Take photographs only with permission. Use the same checklist for every property and separate physical impressions from title, approval, mortgage, tax, and other documentary questions that require verification.
