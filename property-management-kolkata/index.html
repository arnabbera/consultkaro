Complete Property Management Support

ConsultKaro provides a local coordination point for property owners who need structured assistance with occupancy, tenants, documentation, communication, visits, and recurring property requirements in Kolkata.

Who This Service Is For

  • Owners living outside Kolkata or away from the property
  • Owners preparing a residential property for rent
  • Clients needing help coordinating tenants and documentation
  • Families managing an inherited or temporarily vacant property
  • Owners who want a defined contact for routine property matters

Services May Include

Property Preparation

Requirement discussion, condition observations, photography coordination, readiness checklist, and planning for repairs or presentation.

Tenant Coordination

Requirement matching, enquiry handling, visit scheduling, communication, term coordination, and move-in planning.

Documentation Workflow

Collection and organisation of agreed information, rental-agreement coordination, inventory planning, and execution readiness.

Ongoing Assistance

Periodic communication, visit coordination, maintenance requirement reporting, and owner-approved vendor coordination.

How the Process Works

  1. Initial discussion: Confirm the property, location, current condition, owner’s objectives, and expected service scope.
  2. Property review: Identify practical requirements, available information, and matters needing specialist assistance.
  3. Action plan: Agree responsibilities, approvals, communication method, likely expenses, and timeline.
  4. Coordination: Carry out the agreed activities and keep the owner informed.
  5. Ongoing review: Adjust the support according to occupancy and property requirements.

What Is Not Automatically Included

Legal opinions, title verification, technical inspection, valuation, repairs, regulated brokerage activity, tax advice, or financial services are not assumed to be included. Any such requirement should be separately agreed with an appropriately qualified professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can owners living outside Kolkata use this service?

Yes. The scope can be designed around local coordination, reporting, tenant communication, and owner approvals.

Does ConsultKaro directly carry out repairs?

ConsultKaro may coordinate an approved requirement. The vendor, quotation, payment, quality responsibility, and approval process must be separately confirmed.

Can I select only tenant coordination?

Yes. Services can be limited to a defined requirement rather than ongoing management.

Are legal checks included?

Not automatically. A formal legal opinion or title-related work requires an appropriately qualified professional and a separately confirmed scope.
