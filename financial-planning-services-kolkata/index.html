Goal-Based Financial Planning Discussions

Property decisions affect savings, borrowing, monthly cash flow, emergency reserves, insurance, and long-term priorities. ConsultKaro helps clients organise these considerations before taking action.

Who May Find This Useful

  • Families estimating whether a property is affordable
  • Buyers comparing down payment, borrowing, and recurring costs
  • Property owners planning maintenance and vacancy reserves
  • Individuals organising savings around medium- and long-term goals
  • Clients who need referral to an appropriately regulated professional

Discussion Areas

Goals and Priorities

Identify property, family, education, retirement, emergency, and other objectives together with their likely time horizons.

Cash-Flow Planning

Consider income, regular expenses, liabilities, possible instalments, maintenance, taxes, and realistic reserves.

Property Affordability

Compare the purchase price with down payment, borrowing cost, recurring charges, repairs, registration-related expenses, and liquidity needs.

Professional Referral

Identify when regulated investment, loan, insurance, tax, legal, or other specialist advice should be obtained.

Planning Process

  1. Understand objectives: Record the purpose, time horizon, priorities, and major financial commitments.
  2. Review available information: Consider income, expenditure, savings, liabilities, and expected property costs.
  3. Identify gaps and trade-offs: Examine affordability, liquidity, reserves, and conflicting goals.
  4. Prepare questions: Organise the matters that should be discussed with lenders, tax professionals, or regulated advisers.
  5. Review periodically: Revisit the plan when income, markets, goals, or property circumstances change.
