What to Share Before Starting a Property Search

A clear requirement helps a property consultant shortlist more relevant options and reduces unnecessary visits.

A property search becomes more productive when the requirement is specific. Before asking for options, prepare a short brief that a consultant can understand quickly.

Your Property Brief

  • Renting or buying
  • Preferred locations and acceptable alternatives
  • Maximum working budget
  • Property type, approximate size, and room requirement
  • Move-in or possession timeline
  • Parking, lift, furnishing, access, and occupancy needs

Separate Essentials from Preferences

Mark each requirement as essential, preferred, or optional. This allows the search to remain flexible without losing the features that matter most.

Discuss the Complete Cost

Depending on the transaction, consider deposits, recurring maintenance, moving, repair, financing, professional-verification, and registration-related expenses—not only the advertised rent or price.

Keep Records

Save listing links, photographs, visit notes, quoted terms, and contact details. Avoid sharing sensitive original documents unless required and agreed.
