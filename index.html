FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common client questions before you book a consultation.

Which property registration services do you provide?

We assist with property and document search, agreement and deed drafting coordination, valuation, registration preparation and mutation-related follow-up.

Can I request only one part of the registration process?

Yes. You can request assistance for a specific stage such as search, agreement drafting, deed preparation, valuation, registration or mutation.

Does ConsultKaro guarantee title or registration?

No. Registration depends on the parties, documents, authorities and applicable law. Formal legal opinions or certifications must come from appropriately qualified professionals.
