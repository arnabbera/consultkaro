Property Purchase Roadmap

From Verification to Legal Ownership

The three main stages below help buyers organise due diligence, document the bargain and complete the legal transfer of the property.

1

Verify the Property

Before paying any money, verify the seller’s ownership and examine the property records.

  • Title Deed or previous Sale Deed and ownership chain
  • Encumbrance Certificate and relevant loan or charge searches
  • Approved building plans, sanctions and permissions
  • Property tax receipts and utility bill records

This helps confirm the seller’s title and identify mortgages, disputes or document gaps requiring professional review.

2

Execute the Agreement for Sale

The buyer and seller sign an Agreement for Sale recording the agreed transaction.

  • Final sale price and payment schedule
  • Advance or token amount paid
  • Possession and completion dates
  • Obligations, declarations and default terms for both parties

A properly prepared agreement creates a binding record of the parties’ commitments before the final conveyance.

3

Register the Sale Deed & Take Possession

The final Sale Deed is prepared, executed and presented for registration before the appropriate Sub-Registrar.

  • Pay applicable stamp duty and registration charges
  • Execute and register the final Sale Deed
  • Receive possession and the agreed property documents
  • Complete mutation and transfer utility records afterward

Registration completes the legal conveyance, subject to valid title and compliance with applicable law.
